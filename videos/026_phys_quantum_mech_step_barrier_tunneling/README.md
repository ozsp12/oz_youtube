# Quantum Wave Packet and Step Potential

This folder contains Jupyter notebooks, animations, and supporting material for a YouTube lesson on the numerical evolution of a Gaussian quantum wave packet interacting with a step potential.

The simulation illustrates wave-packet dispersion, reflection, transmission, and tunneling behavior through the time-dependent Schrödinger equation.
